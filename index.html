Shadow.

A combact-driven 2.5D isometric roguelite — architecture, combat, and systems.

Engine Unity 2022.3 LTS
PlatformPC · Windows
Team 8 devs · 3 sub-teams

Shadow is a skill-focused, roguelike game built around a single core mechanic: predicting enemy attacks, parrying them with exquisite timing, and doing devastating counter attacks. While three procedurally generated floors continuously raise the pressure on the player, the roguelike system in which players choose from 23 types of upgrades is designed so that a unique combat style emerges with every round. The internal structure adopts a service- and event-based architecture, where the player, enemy, combat, and UI systems are decoupled so as to function independently from one another. This made it possible for three sub-teams to proceed with work in parallel, achieving development within a span of four sprints throughout the semester.

01 Architecture Services + Systems

A GameManager bootstraps global services and hands scene-scoped gameplay systems to a typed EventService bus. Systems never reference each other directly. Balance and content live entirely in ScriptableObjects, so designers can tune without touching code.

04 Parry Combat The core mechanic

Every enemy attack telegraphs a red ground circle. A percise Parry (tap E) reflects damage and opens a 1.1-second counter window, during which a left-click snaps the player to the target and executes a one shot damage finisher. Guard (hold RMB) is the safer alternative: it absorbs all damage at a stamina cost and opens the same counter window.

10 Meta Progression

Soul Shards — awarded per run from rooms cleared, kills, and boss defeats. They are spent at the Hub for permanent unlocks that expand the upgrade pool (e.g. Fire Enchant, Ice Enchant, Legendary Executioner) rather than granting flat stat boosts. The core challenge stays skill-gated; meta progression widens build variety.
